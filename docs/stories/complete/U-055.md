# U-055: Define immersion trade-off and surplus-allocation insights

## Objective

Define how Range History should explain the trade-off between exporting surplus
solar and diverting surplus into immersion so users can understand:

- how much surplus energy went to hot water versus export
- what export value was likely forgone by sending that energy to immersion
- why this is a trade-off, not automatically a mistake

This story should make immersion diversion legible inside the wider `FE-015`
financial model without turning it into prescriptive advice.

## Scope

- Define the first-pass immersion trade-off layer for Range History as a
  supporting interpretation within `Energy value`
- Define the first-pass immersion metrics, including:
  - `Immersion diverted`
  - `Exported`
  - `Immersion vs export split`
  - `Potential export credit foregone`
- Define the intended meaning, formula basis, and user-facing explanation for
  each metric
- Define how these insights should be framed so the product presents immersion
  as a trade-off rather than a warning or mistake
- Define what belongs in the first implementation versus what should be
  explicitly deferred
- Define the presentation direction clearly enough for `P-055`, including:
  - selected-period summary comparison
  - split/ratio view
  - trend comparison over time
  - explanatory callout language

## First-Pass Metric Model

### 1. Core immersion trade-off metrics

1. `Immersion diverted`
   Meaning: the amount of surplus solar energy routed to immersion in the
   selected period.
   Formula basis: summed immersion-diverted kWh for the covered days.

2. `Exported`
   Meaning: the amount of surplus solar energy exported to the grid in the
   selected period.
   Formula basis: summed exported kWh for the covered days.

3. `Immersion vs export split`
   Meaning: how the user's surplus solar energy was allocated between hot water
   diversion and grid export in the selected period.
   Formula basis: `immersion diverted kWh` and `exported kWh` shown as an
   absolute and percentage split of `immersion diverted + exported`.

4. `Potential export credit foregone`
   Meaning: the export value that would likely have been earned if the energy
   sent to immersion had instead been exported.
   Formula basis: `immersion diverted kWh × applicable export tariff
   treatment for the selected period`.

### 2. Placement within the Range History model

These metrics belong in the `Energy value` section introduced by `U-054`.

They are supporting interpretation metrics, not top-row hero totals. The hero
story for the page remains:

- total solar value
- self-consumed versus export value split
- repayments
- net solar position

The immersion trade-off layer should help explain how surplus solar was used,
not replace the page's primary financial story.

## Metric Classification Rules

### Measured totals

These come directly from stored energy totals:

- immersion diverted kWh
- exported kWh

### Tariff-priced estimates

These depend on tariff pricing but not on a no-solar counterfactual:

- potential export credit foregone

### Deferred estimates

These should not be included in the first pass unless a trustworthy model is
defined:

- estimated hot-water value gained
- immersion-versus-other-heating ROI comparisons

## Framing Rules

1. Immersion diversion must be framed as a `trade-off`.
   The product should make clear that sending surplus solar to hot water may be
   useful, but it also means that energy did not earn export credit.

2. `Potential export credit foregone` is an opportunity-cost metric, not a
   judgment metric.
   The wording must not imply that export would always have been the better
   choice.

3. The first-pass model should avoid pretending to know the full value of hot
   water unless the product can clearly model what heating cost was actually
   displaced.

4. The page should help users notice their immersion/export behaviour, but it
   should not tell them how to control appliances or optimize settings in this
   story.

## Presentation Direction

The first pass should be clear enough to build without locking the final visual
packaging. The implementation should support:

1. `Selected-period summary`
   A concise explanation of how much surplus went to immersion versus export,
   plus the likely export value foregone.

2. `Split/ratio view`
   A simple visual showing the surplus allocation between immersion and export
   for the selected period.

3. `Trend comparison`
   A month-by-month or period-by-period comparison showing whether surplus is
   more often being exported or diverted to immersion over time.

4. `Explanatory callout`
   Plain-English copy that explains the trade-off in user terms, for example:
   "You diverted X kWh to immersion and exported Y kWh. If the immersed energy
   had been exported instead, it would likely have earned about EUR Z in export
   credit."

## Explicit Deferrals

The first implementation should defer:

- estimating the financial value of hot water gained unless a trustworthy
  displacement baseline is defined
- appliance-control recommendations
- notifications or automation about immersion behavior
- a claim about whether immersion was the "right" choice

## Out of Scope

- Full water-heating economics modeling without a stronger baseline
- Personalized advice about changing immersion usage
- Battery or other appliance surplus-routing strategy
- Replacing the hero financial narrative defined in `U-054`

## Acceptance Criteria

- Range History has a documented first-pass immersion trade-off model
- The story defines the intended meaning and formula basis for immersion
  diverted, exported, immersion/export split, and potential export credit
  foregone
- The story makes clear that immersion diversion has opportunity cost relative
  to export without implying it is always the worse choice
- The story explicitly places immersion insights inside `Energy value` as
  supporting interpretation rather than a hero KPI replacement
- The story clearly defers hot-water-value claims unless a trustworthy
  displacement model exists
- The implementation direction is clear enough to build without reopening the
  conceptual model

## Dependencies

- `U-054`
- Existing daily summary fields for immersion and export
- Export tariff data for the selected period
