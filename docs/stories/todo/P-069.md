# P-069: Build monthly and yearly performance roundup notifications

## Objective

Send recurring period-end summaries that help users understand how their system
performed over the last month or year and encourage them to return to the app.

## Scope

- Implement the first recurring monthly and yearly roundup notifications
- Reuse the digest requirements from `U-016`
- Include a concise mix of:
  - top-line generation / consumption / solar-value outcomes
  - at least one standout highlight or comparison
  - a clear CTA back into the app
- Respect saved notification preferences and period eligibility rules
- Ensure roundups only send when enough data coverage exists to keep the summary
  trustworthy

## Out of Scope

- Daily digest delivery
- AI-authored narratives or individualized recommendations
- Rich multi-email campaigns per period

## Acceptance Criteria

- Monthly and yearly roundup notifications can be generated and sent on a
  recurring schedule
- Roundup content uses trustworthy period metrics with appropriate coverage
  gating
- Users can disable recurring roundups through notification preferences
- The summary copy and CTA encourage the user to revisit the app meaningfully
  rather than acting as a generic newsletter

## Dependencies

- `FE-018`
- `U-016`
- Existing period-summary data and notification-preference infrastructure
