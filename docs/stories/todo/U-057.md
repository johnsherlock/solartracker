# U-057: Add “Include estimates for missing data” to Range History

## Objective

Give users a clear, explicit control on `Range History` to include or exclude
estimated values for missing historical days, and ensure estimated days are
obvious everywhere they appear.

## Scope

- Add a Range History toggle such as:
  - `Measured data only`
  - `Include estimates for missing data`
- When estimates are enabled:
  - include estimated days in graphs and period totals
  - visually distinguish estimated days from measured days
  - clearly label period totals that include estimated data
- When estimates are disabled:
  - keep current measured-data-only behavior
- Keep the dedicated Historical Day screen unchanged for missing days
- Add explanatory product copy so users understand what the toggle does and
  what estimates mean

## Out of Scope

- Designing the estimation algorithm itself
- Creating synthetic Historical Day telemetry
- Making every product surface use estimates automatically

## Acceptance Criteria

- Range History exposes a clear opt-in/out control for estimated missing days
- Estimated days are visually distinct in charts or other rendered range data
- Period totals that include estimates are explicitly labeled as estimate-aware
- Disabling the toggle restores measured-data-only range behavior
- Clicking through to a missing Historical Day still shows the normal missing
  day experience rather than a synthetic day-detail view
