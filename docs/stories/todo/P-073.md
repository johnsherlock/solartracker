# P-073: Investigate natural-language Historical Day storytelling

## Objective

Explore whether the Historical Day page and future daily roundup notifications
can use a more natural, less templated day-story style without introducing a
heavy or brittle AI dependency.

## Scope

- Review the current Historical Day story output and identify where it feels
  repetitive or obviously rule-based
- Investigate lightweight local approaches for richer day-summary generation,
  including:
  - small on-device/local LLM runtimes that are realistic for app-side or
    operator-side use
  - template-plus-variation systems that may achieve enough day-to-day variety
    without full generative inference
  - hybrid approaches where deterministic facts are extracted first and
    natural-language rendering happens in a second pass
- Assess whether the same story-generation approach could sensibly power:
  - the Historical Day article
  - daily roundup notifications
  - future monthly/yearly digest surfaces
- Document data-shaping requirements for a future generator input model, such as
  ranked highlights, trust signals, tariff context, solar/export mix, and
  weather/daylight context
- Recommend a first implementation direction with tradeoffs across:
  - runtime weight
  - cost
  - latency
  - privacy
  - consistency / hallucination risk
  - tone variety

## Out of Scope

- Shipping a production AI summary system in this story
- Adding external hosted LLM dependencies by default
- Replacing the current Historical Day story immediately without a chosen
  implementation path

## Acceptance Criteria

- A short decision-oriented write-up exists comparing at least two realistic
  approaches for more natural Historical Day summaries
- The write-up recommends whether the first pass should be:
  - deterministic templating with better variation
  - local/on-device generation
  - server-side generation behind a future explicit dependency
- The write-up states whether the chosen direction can also support daily
  roundup notifications
- Any follow-on implementation stories needed are identified explicitly

## Dependencies

- `FE-003`
- Existing Historical Day story implementation
- Existing notification/digest roadmap items such as `FE-018`
