Setup · Tariffs

Real tariffs give real ROI estimates.

Every euro figure on the dashboard runs through your real tariff schedule. Solar savings are calculated taking your day, night, peak and weekend rates into consideration. Set it once and every kWh, past and future, is priced correctly.

  • A flexible tariff editor captures every nuance of your contract.
  • Switched providers? Rates changed mid-contract? Add a new version of your tariffs with start and end dates.
  • Made a correction? Your historical data is automatically re-priced.
  • Export rates and standing charges included — so net bill comparisons are real, not approximate.
  • The further back you can go, the more accurately your ROI is calculated.
